## Product rules that must hold everywhere (copy, UI, emails, PDFs)

1. Three service levels only: **Libre-service (290 DH HT)**, **Vérification assistée (+79 DH HT)**, **Validation Avocat (990 DH HT)**. Lawyer-required cases keep the existing routed price. Domiciliation does not exist anywhere. Company formation (SARL) is out of scope.
2. The platform is never described as AI, "IA", "intelligence artificielle", "généré par l'IA" or similar. The assisted check is called "vérification automatisée" and documents are "préparés à partir de modèles approuvés". Search the whole repo, both languages, including emails, PDFs, admin screens and lawyer screens.
3. The platform never gives legal advice and never claims blanket legal validity or "conformité" of a document. Use wording like "modèle basé sur la loi X" and "à valider selon votre situation".
4. The lawyer signs a separate attestation, never the contract itself. Payment for the lawyer tier is captured only after the lawyer validates.
5. The client never chooses the workflow route. The routing engine decides. Do not weaken this.
6. Nothing on the site may look like an official State document. No "Royaume du Maroc" header, no coat of arms, no official-looking seals.
